# What the logo means

The mark is one horse carrying one rider, drawn three times.

## The mapping

- **The horse is the measurement framework.** It carries everything, it sits underneath everyone, nobody looks at it while things are going well, and if it goes lame nothing arrives no matter how good the rider is.
- **The rider is the product data scientist.** Routing and judgment: which method the question deserves, what the answer would cost, how much confidence it can carry.
- **The three positions are one question moving through the map**, from description to cause to decision. The artwork is a motion sequence, one rider drawn three times, not three separate riders.
- **The destination is a decision.** Growth is what accumulates when enough of them are right.

Same image, no forcing, and it still says the thing it most needs to say.

## The rule this follows

A metaphor should carry exactly one idea and stay decorative for everything else. Here the one carried idea is: **nothing arrives without the horse.** Everything else is decoration.

Systems where every visual element has to mean something turn into quizzes, and people stop using them.

The tell that a metaphor is being forced: you start shopping for a taxonomy that fits the picture instead of picking the picture that fits the taxonomy.

## Practical note

The horse gallops to the left, so the motion sequence reads right to left, while the method map reads left to right. Flip the logo if the two ever appear side by side.
